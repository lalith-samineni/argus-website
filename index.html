THE DEVICE

One small box.
The whole system
is inside it.

A Raspberry Pi 5, a camera module and a printed enclosure. The models run on the Pi itself, so the camera keeps working whether or not it can reach the internet — it just can't text you until it can.

  • Argus unitPi 5 + camera, assembled and flashed
  • EnclosurePETG, printed, Rev C
  • MountsDash pad, wall plate, ¼-20 for any tripod
  • PowerUSB-C, 12 V car adapter included
  • AppiOS, free with the device
HOW IT SEES

Four signals, computed together,
dozens of times a second.

Every stage runs on the Pi. Frames are read into memory, scored, and dropped — they are never written to disk, and there is no upload step to turn off.

It sees everything.
It sends nothing but the alert.

This is the part people check first. A camera in your parent's kitchen is only acceptable if nobody — including us — can ever watch it.

SETUP

Out of the box and watching
in under an hour.

The unit ships assembled and flashed. There is nothing to compile and no model to download.

  1. 1

    Mount it and give it power

    Dash pad for the car, wall plate for a room, ¼-20 boss for any tripod you already own. USB-C in, status LED on.

  2. 2

    Pair it in the app

    Open the iOS app, scan the code on the back, join it to your Wi-Fi. Name the unit — “Car”, “Mom's kitchen” — and it shows up on your home screen.

  3. 3

    Pick a profile, or describe the job

    Choose Drive, Care or Watch, then adjust sensitivity. For anything else, type what you want watched and let Argus configure itself.

Building your own from parts instead? argus deploy --all takes raw hardware to a working unit in a single command.

Am I being charged now?

No. Reserving puts you on the list and nothing else. You pay when we have a unit built for you and you confirm you still want it.

Do I have to subscribe?

Only to receive alerts on your phone. The detection itself runs on the device whether you subscribe or not — but a camera that notices something and can't tell you isn't much use, so most people do.

Can you or anyone else see my video?

No, and not because of a policy. The frames are scored in memory and dropped; they are never written to disk and never sent anywhere. The only thing that leaves is a short text record of what happened and when.

Does it work without internet?

Detection does — it is all on-device. In the car it will still sound the in-cab nudge with no signal at all. Pushing the alert to your phone needs a connection, and it sends it as soon as it has one.

Can one device cover more than one job?

One at a time. Switch a unit from Drive to Care from the app in a couple of seconds — people who want the car and the house covered at once buy two.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Send it back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the device, and we'll cancel the subscription the same day.
